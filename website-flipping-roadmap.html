The 7-Phase Roadmap

1

Pick a Niche That Sells

Day 1 30 minutes
  • Choose from high-conversion niches (see table below)
  • Avoid YMYL: health, finance, crypto, gambling
  • Sub-niche it: not "pets" → "reptile care for beginners"
2

Buy a Clean Domain

Day 1 $10 – $30
  • .com only — others tank resale value
  • 2 – 3 words, brandable, 8 – 15 chars, no hyphens or numbers
  • Sources: Namecheap, GoDaddy auctions, Expireddomains.net
  • Aged domains with backlinks = $50 – $200, but adds value at sale
3

Build the Site

Day 2 – 4 4 – 6 hours
  • Stack: WordPress + Astra/Kadence theme on Hostinger ($3/mo) or Cloudways ($10/mo)
  • 15 – 30 articles, 1,000 – 2,000 words each (Claude-generated, human-edited)
  • Affiliate links: Amazon Associates + 1 niche program
  • Email opt-in form (Brevo/MailerLite free tier)
  • Logo (Canva), favicon, About / Privacy / Terms pages
4

Add Trust Signals

Day 4 – 5 1 hour
  • Connect Google Analytics (2.8× more views, 2.4× more watchers per Flippa)
  • Set up Google Search Console + submit sitemap
  • Build 5 – 10 free backlinks (web 2.0s, profile links, Reddit comments)
  • Take screenshots of clean dashboards for the listing
5

Let It Age

Week 2 – 6 passive
  • Site sits 4 – 6 weeks while it indexes — this is critical
  • Even 50 – 200 monthly visitors can 2 – 3× your sale price
  • This is where you build site #2 and #3 in parallel
6

List Like a Pitch

Week 6 2 hours
  • Auction format, 7-day duration, BIN price + lower starting bid
  • Listing must include: niche, monetization path, screenshots, "buyer's roadmap" of next steps
  • Be honest with stats — Flippa verifies via Analytics
  • Use Flippa's free valuation tool as a sanity check
7

Sell, Then Systemize

Week 7+ ongoing
  • First flip goal: break even or small profit, learn the platform
  • Replicate the niche/stack that worked
  • Build 3 – 5 in parallel, stagger listings every 2 weeks
  • Volume math: 1 sale/wk × $640 avg net = $2,560/mo

Do This / Avoid This

✓ Do

  • Connect Google Analytics before listing
  • Let sites age 4 – 6 weeks for indexing
  • Build 3 – 5 in parallel, stagger listings
  • Use the same stack every time (templates win)
  • Write listings as a roadmap for the buyer
  • Stick to .com, brandable domains
  • Screenshot everything — buyers want proof

✗ Avoid

  • YMYL niches (health, finance, legal advice)
  • Crypto, gambling, MMO niches — saturated & flagged
  • Inflating traffic — Flippa verifies, gets you banned
  • One-off flips (margin's too thin without volume)
  • Custom-coded sites (buyers want WordPress)
  • .io, .co, .net domains for spec builds
  • Listing same week the site goes live (no aging)
