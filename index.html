Common Questions

Answered before you ask.

What's your typical project timeline? ＋

Most builds ship within 2 to 4 weeks. Simple funnels and single automations are usually done in 5 to 10 days. Larger web app builds run 4 to 8 weeks depending on scope. You get a firm timeline in your quote before we start.

How do you charge for your work? ＋

Fixed price per project. You get a written quote after the discovery call and that number does not change unless you add new scope. For ongoing clients I also offer monthly retainers for continuous automation and app work.

Do you work with businesses outside your local area? ＋

Yes. Most of my work is fully remote. I collaborate with clients across timezones using Slack, Loom, and scheduled calls. Location is not a factor.

What if I don't know exactly what I need? ＋

That is what the free 20 minute consultation is for. Bring the problem, I bring the solutions. Most clients walk out of that call with 2 or 3 clear options and a rough sense of what each would cost.

Can you work with my existing tools and systems? ＋

Almost always. I work across GoHighLevel, Make, Zapier, n8n, WordPress, Stripe, Airtable, Notion, Slack, Google Workspace, and most modern web frameworks. If your stack is not listed, ask and I will tell you honestly.

Do you provide ongoing support after launch? ＋

Every build includes 30 days of post launch support for bugs, tweaks, and questions. After that, you can either self manage using the documentation I provide, or continue with a monthly support retainer.
