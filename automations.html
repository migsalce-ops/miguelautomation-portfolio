Speed to Lead

Contact every new lead in under 60 seconds.

What it does: The moment a new lead comes in from a form, ad, Facebook page, or landing page, they get an instant text, email, and (optionally) a call from your team. No lag. No lead going cold.

Why it matters: Leads contacted within 5 minutes are up to 21x more likely to convert than leads contacted an hour later. Most businesses respond in hours or days. This closes that gap.

Document Processing

Read, extract, and route documents automatically.

What it does: AI powered intake for contracts, invoices, intake forms, and PDFs. The system reads the document, pulls out the key data (names, dates, amounts, IDs), and pushes it straight into your CRM, spreadsheet, or the right person's inbox.

Why it matters: Cuts hours of manual data entry per week, kills copy paste errors, and gives your admin team back their afternoons.

Follow Up Nurture Sequence

Keep every lead warm until they're ready to buy.

What it does: Multi touch nurture sequences across email, SMS, and voicemail drops. Personalized timing, branching based on lead behaviour, and automatic pausing the moment a lead books or replies.

Why it matters: Most sales happen after the fifth touchpoint. Manual follow up almost always breaks down by touch two. Automated nurture keeps working while you sleep.
